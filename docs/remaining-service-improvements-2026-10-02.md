# Remaining service guides — 2 October 2026

Emergency plumbing, burst-pipe repair, gas and commercial plumbing now each
include two additional customer decision sections. They distinguish emergency
authority assistance from routine booking, assessment from permanent repairs,
pipe work from reinstatement, job-specific authorisation from company claims,
and operational requirements from guaranteed availability.

Existing FAQs are retained. Official sources are now linked on these four guides:
VICSES when-to-call, Victorian Government power safety, Energy Safe Victoria gas
emergencies, and Building and Plumbing Commission practitioner guidance. Checked
on 2 October 2026. No individual credentials are represented as verified.

No prices, job histories, warranties, response times or opening hours are invented.
No DIY gas, electrical or hazardous access procedure is introduced. Public URLs,
canonicals, service eligibility and noindex restrictions remain unchanged.
