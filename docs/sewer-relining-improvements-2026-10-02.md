# Sewer repair and pipe relining guidance — 2 October 2026

Added customer decision guidance to the existing shared service pages. Sewer
repair enquiries now explain requesting inspection findings, distinguishing a
symptom from a confirmed defect, comparing alternatives and approving variations.
Relining enquiries explain requesting a job-specific proposal, comparing full
scopes and reading actual warranty terms. Existing FAQs and suitability caveats
are retained.

These are questions to discuss before approval, not claims of available equipment,
guaranteed suitability, qualifications, warranties, lifespan, savings or response
times. No new technical safety instructions were added. No new location pages,
URLs, canonical targets or indexing permissions were introduced.

Verification: regression tests, production build, representative live content,
canonical, mobile and indexability checks. Google recrawl, indexing and ranking
changes are not implied by deployment.
